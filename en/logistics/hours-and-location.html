Gongbei Port operates on an extended schedule to accommodate the high volume of daily travelers between mainland China and Macau SAR. Understanding these hours is crucial for planning your crossing.

⚠️ Important Notice

Gongbei Port is NOT open 24 hours. The port closes daily from 1:00 AM to 6:00 AM. For late-night crossings, consider alternative border points like Hengqin Port or the Lotus Bridge.

🚇 Transportation to Port

Zhuhai Side: Direct access via Zhuhai Railway Station (connected to Guangzhou-Shenzhen-Hong Kong Express Rail). Local buses routes 1, 10, 11, 32, 34 stop at Gongbei Port.

Macau Side: Walking distance to Macau Peninsula attractions. Bus routes AP1, 3, 10, 10A, 10B, 10X, 28B, 28C, 28X serve the border area.

🅿️ Parking Facilities

Large parking complexes available on both sides. Zhuhai side offers multi-story parking with hourly rates. Macau side has limited parking; public transport recommended.

Tip: During peak hours (8-10 AM, 6-8 PM), parking fills quickly. Arrive early or use ride-sharing services.

🕐 Peak vs Off-Peak Times

Peak Hours: 7:00-9:30 AM (commuters), 6:00-8:30 PM (return travelers), weekends 10:00 AM-2:00 PM.

Best Times: 10:00 AM-4:00 PM weekdays, late evening after 9:00 PM (but mind closing time).

📍 Key Landmarks

Zhuhai Side: Gongbei Underground Shopping Mall, Zhuhai Railway Station, Lovers' Road coastal path.

Macau Side: Macau Tower visible nearby, Lisboa Casino, historic city center within walking distance.

Planning Your Visit

To ensure a smooth border crossing experience at Gongbei Port, consider these essential planning tips:

  1. Check Holiday Schedules: Verify operating hours before traveling during Chinese or Macau public holidays.
  2. Allow Extra Time: During peak periods, border processing can take 1-2 hours. Add buffer time to your itinerary.
  3. Prepare Documents: Have passport, visa (if required), and any necessary permits ready before reaching immigration counters.
  4. Monitor Real-Time Conditions: Check local news or official channels for unexpected closures or delays.
  5. Consider Alternatives: If Gongbei is crowded, Hengqin Port or the Lotus Bridge may offer faster crossing options.

💡 Pro Tip

Download the official "Gongbei Port" mobile app (available in Chinese) for real-time queue information and service updates. Third-party travel apps like WeChat Mini Programs also provide wait time estimates.

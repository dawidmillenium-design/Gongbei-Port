⏰ Operating Hours & Timing

No, Gongbei Port is NOT open 24 hours. The port operates daily from 6:00 AM to 1:00 AM (next day), closing for 5 hours each night.

If you need to cross during the closed hours (1:00 AM - 6:00 AM), consider these alternatives:

  • Hengqin Port: Open 24 hours, located in western Zhuhai
  • Lotus Bridge: 24-hour vehicle crossing (pedestrians not permitted)
  • Ferry terminals: Some operate late-night services

Tip: During Chinese public holidays, Gongbei Port may extend hours. Check official announcements before traveling.

Crossing time varies significantly based on several factors:

Time of Day Estimated Time
Off-peak (10 AM - 4 PM weekdays) 15-30 minutes
Morning peak (7-9 AM) 45-60 minutes
Evening peak (6-8 PM) 45-90 minutes
Weekends/Holidays 60-120+ minutes

Factors affecting time:

  • Using automated facial recognition vs. manual counters
  • Nationality (some passports require additional verification)
  • Amount of luggage (may trigger customs inspection)
  • Special events or security alerts

For the shortest wait times, aim for:

  • Weekdays: 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM (mid-morning to mid-afternoon)
  • Late evening: After 9:00 PM (but ensure you cross before 1:00 AM closing)
  • Avoid: 7:00-9:30 AM (commuters), 6:00-8:30 PM (return travelers), weekend mornings

Pro tip: Tuesday through Thursday typically have the lowest traffic. Monday mornings and Friday evenings are particularly busy.

🛂 Visa & Document Requirements

Visa requirements depend on your nationality:

Visa-Free Entry (30-90 days): Citizens of over 70 countries including United States, United Kingdom, European Union members, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Japan, South Korea, Singapore, Brazil, and Portugal.

Visa Required: Citizens of countries not on the visa-free list must obtain a Macau visa in advance from a Chinese embassy/consulate or authorized agency.

Chinese Citizens: Need a valid Mainland Travel Permit for Macau (Exit-Entry Permit) with appropriate endorsement.

Important: Always verify current requirements with official sources before traveling, as regulations can change.

Essential documents for crossing Gongbei Port:

  • Valid Passport: Must have at least 6 months validity beyond your intended stay
  • Visa/Permit: If required for your nationality
  • Completed Entry/Exit Cards: Available at the port or on transport
  • Proof of Onward Travel: Return ticket or confirmation of exit from Macau/China
  • Hotel Reservation: May be requested by immigration officers
  • Sufficient Funds: Evidence you can support yourself during stay

Recommended backups:

  • Digital copies of all documents saved on phone/cloud
  • Physical photocopies separate from originals
  • Emergency contact numbers for your embassy/consulate

No, Macau does not offer visa on arrival at Gongbei Port. Visas must be obtained in advance if your nationality requires one.

However, Macau offers a relatively generous visa-free policy for tourists from many countries. Check if your nationality qualifies for visa-free entry before applying for a visa.

For Chinese citizens: The Exit-Entry Permit for Macau must be obtained in mainland China before travel. It cannot be issued at the border.

🚶 Crossing Process & Procedures

Yes! Gongbei Port features advanced automated facial recognition channels for faster processing.

Eligibility requirements:

  • Holder of biometric e-passport from eligible country
  • Aged between 7 and 70 years old
  • No previous immigration violations
  • First-time users may need to register biometric data at manual counters

How to use:

  1. Approach automated gate when green light is on
  2. Place passport face-down on scanner
  3. Look directly at camera (remove glasses, masks, hats)
  4. Wait for match confirmation (5-10 seconds)
  5. Proceed through gate when it opens

If facial recognition fails twice, proceed to manual counter for assistance.

Yes! Gongbei Port is primarily designed for pedestrian crossing. After clearing immigration and customs on either side, you can simply walk into the adjacent city.

The walking route:

  • Zhuhai side: Enter through main terminal building
  • Clear Chinese exit immigration
  • Walk through transit corridor (duty-free shops available)
  • Clear Macau entry immigration
  • Exit into Macau Peninsula

Total walking distance within the port complex is approximately 300-500 meters, taking 5-10 minutes excluding queue times.

If you lose your passport at or near Gongbei Port:

  1. Report immediately to the nearest police station (on either side of the border)
  2. Contact your embassy/consulate - Macau has consular services for many nations; Zhuhai has the broader Guangzhou consular district
  3. File a police report - Required for emergency travel document issuance
  4. Apply for emergency travel document - Your embassy can issue temporary papers for return travel
  5. Allow extra time - This process can take 1-3 business days minimum

Prevention: Always keep digital copies of your passport stored separately (email, cloud, phone). Carry a physical photocopy separate from the original.

🚫 Prohibited & Restricted Items

Food restrictions are strictly enforced at Gongbei Port:

Generally Allowed:

  • Packaged, non-perishable snacks (chips, cookies, candy)
  • Commercially sealed beverages
  • Small quantities for personal consumption

Restricted or Prohibited:

  • ❌ Fresh meat and meat products
  • ❌ Dairy products (milk, cheese, yogurt)
  • ❌ Fresh fruits and vegetables
  • ❌ Live animals or animal products
  • ❌ Seeds and plants without phytosanitary certificates

Important: When in doubt, declare food items to customs officers. Undeclared prohibited food can result in fines up to MOP/CNY 50,000.

Cash declaration requirements:

Entering/Leaving Macau: Must declare if carrying more than MOP 120,000 (or equivalent in foreign currency).

Entering/Leaving Mainland China: Must declare if carrying more than USD 5,000 (or equivalent) in foreign currency, or more than CNY 20,000 in Chinese yuan.

Consequences of non-declaration:

  • Confiscation of undeclared amounts exceeding limits
  • Fines ranging from 10% to 50% of undeclared amount
  • Potential criminal investigation for large sums

Tip: Use ATMs at the port rather than carrying large cash amounts. Both sides have international ATM networks.

🚗 Transportation & Access

Multiple transportation options to Gongbei Port:

🚄 Train (Recommended):

  • Zhuhai Railway Station connects directly to the port complex
  • High-speed rail from Guangzhou (1 hour), Shenzhen (1.5 hours)
  • Exit station and follow signs to border crossing

🚌 Bus:

  • Routes 1, 10, 11, 32, 34 stop at Gongbei Port
  • Fare: CNY 1-2
  • Frequency: Every 10-15 minutes

🚕 Taxi/Ride-share:

  • Taxis widely available throughout Zhuhai
  • Didi Chuxing (Chinese Uber) operates in Zhuhai
  • Drop-off point directly at port entrance

Yes! Taxi stands are located immediately outside the Macau exit of Gongbei Port.

Macau Taxi Information:

  • Payment: Cash (MOP, HKD, CNY accepted), some accept credit cards
  • Flag fall: MOP 19 (first 1.6 km)
  • Additional: MOP 2 per 240m thereafter
  • Luggage: MOP 3 per piece in trunk

Ride-hailing Apps:

  • Macau Taxi App (official government app)
  • Uber does NOT operate in Macau

Popular destinations from port:

  • Lisboa Casino: ~10 minutes, MOP 40-50
  • Macau Tower: ~15 minutes, MOP 50-60
  • Cotai Strip (Venetian): ~20 minutes, MOP 70-80
  • Macau Airport: ~25 minutes, MOP 80-100

🏢 Port Facilities & Services

Yes! Comprehensive financial services are available:

ATMs:

  • Located on both Zhuhai and Macau sides
  • Accept Visa, Mastercard, UnionPay, JCB
  • Dispense CNY (Zhuhai side) or MOP/HKD (Macau side)
  • Withdrawal fees apply (check with your bank)

Currency Exchange Counters:

  • Multiple exchange bureaus in transit area
  • Currencies: CNY, MOP, HKD, USD, EUR, JPY, etc.
  • Rates typically less favorable than banks/ATMs
  • Open during port operating hours

Best Practice: Withdraw local currency from ATMs for better rates. Notify your bank of travel plans to avoid card blocks.

Yes! Left luggage facilities operate on both sides of the border:

Zhuhai Side:

  • Location: Near main entrance, Level 1
  • Hours: 6:00 AM - 1:00 AM
  • Rates: CNY 20-40 per item per day (size-dependent)

Macau Side:

  • Location: Border Gate Transportation Center
  • Hours: 6:00 AM - 1:00 AM
  • Rates: MOP 30-50 per item per day

Restrictions:

  • Maximum storage period: 30 days
  • Valuables, electronics, perishables not accepted
  • Passport/ID required for retrieval

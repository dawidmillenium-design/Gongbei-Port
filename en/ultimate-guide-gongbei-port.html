1. Overview of Gongbei Port

Gongbei Port (拱北口岸) is the world's busiest land border crossing, connecting Zhuhai in mainland China with the Macau Special Administrative Region. Handling over 400,000 travelers daily, it serves as the primary gateway for tourists, commuters, and shoppers moving between the two regions.

Unlike other borders that require vehicle transfers, Gongbei is a pedestrian-only crossing. You must walk through the immigration terminal, which spans both the Chinese and Macanese sides.

Key Fact: The entire crossing process typically takes 30–60 minutes depending on queue times, but can extend to 2+ hours during peak holidays.

2. Operating Hours & Best Times

Gongbei Port is NOT open 24 hours. It has specific operating windows:

  • Standard Hours: 06:00 AM to 01:00 AM (Next Day).
  • Peak Seasons: During major holidays (Chinese New Year, Golden Week), hours may be extended, but queues are significantly longer.

Best Times to Cross

  • Low Traffic: 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM (Weekdays).
  • Avoid: 8:00 AM – 9:30 AM (Commuter rush) and 6:00 PM – 9:00 PM (Return traffic).
  • Weekends: Expect heavy crowds all day; arrive before 10 AM or after 8 PM.

3. Visa & Entry Requirements

Requirements depend on your nationality:

  • Mainland Chinese Residents: Need a valid Exit-Entry Permit for Travelling to and from Hong Kong and Macau (往来港澳通行证) with an endorsement.
  • International Tourists: Most nationalities receive a Visa on Arrival (actually an entry permit) for Macau upon exiting China. Check if your country is eligible for visa-free entry (typically 30-90 days).
  • Re-entry to China: Ensure you have a valid Chinese visa with multiple entries if you plan to return to Zhuhai.
Warning: Ensure your passport has at least 6 months validity remaining. Denied entry due to expired documents is common.

4. Step-by-Step Crossing Process

  1. Arrival: Arrive at the Gongbei plaza. Follow signs for "Exit to Macau" (出境).
  2. Security Check: Pass through an initial airport-style security scan. Remove laptops and large liquids.
  3. China Immigration: Queue for Mainland China exit inspection. Use either the manual counter (passport stamp) or the e-channel (if eligible).
  4. Walk the Bridge: Walk through the covered walkway (approx. 5-10 mins) to the Macau side.
  5. Macau Immigration: Queue for Macau entry inspection. Fill out an arrival card if required (often waived for tourists now).
  6. Arrival in Macau: Exit into the Macau plaza where taxis, buses, and casino shuttles await.

5. Using Facial Recognition Channels

Both China and Macau now utilize advanced e-channels with facial recognition technology.

Eligibility

  • Mainland residents with biometric permits.
  • Macau residents.
  • Select international frequent travelers registered in the program.

Tips for Success

  • Remove hats, sunglasses, and masks.
  • Look directly at the camera lens.
  • If you have changed your appearance significantly, use the manual counter.

6. Duty-Free Shopping

Located on the Zhuhai side (before exiting China) and the Macau side, duty-free shops offer tobacco, alcohol, perfume, and cosmetics.

  • Location: Inside the secure zone after China exit immigration.
  • Payment: WeChat Pay, Alipay, UnionPay, and Cash (CNY/MOP).
  • Limits: Be aware of customs limits when entering your final destination (e.g., 200 cigarettes, 1L alcohol for many countries).

7. Transport Options After Crossing

From Macau Side

  • Casino Shuttles (Free): Located immediately outside the exit. Lines can be long for popular casinos (Venetian, Grand Lisboa).
  • Public Bus: Multiple routes to Taipa, Cotai, and Coloane. Cost: 6 MOP.
  • Taxi: Black taxis available. Surge pricing applies during peak hours/rain.

8. Frequently Asked Questions

Is Gongbei Port open 24 hours?

No, Gongbei Port operates from 06:00 AM to 01:00 AM. For late-night crossings, consider the Hengqin Port (open 24h) or the Lotus Bridge.


Can I bring food across the border?

Generally, fresh fruits, vegetables, and meat products are prohibited from entering Macau. Packaged snacks are usually fine.

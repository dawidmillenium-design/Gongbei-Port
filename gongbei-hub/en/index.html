Quick FAQ

No, Gongbei Port is not open 24 hours. Standard operating hours are typically 6:00 AM to 1:00 AM, but these vary by season and day of the week. Always check our hours page for current schedules before traveling.

Visa requirements depend on your nationality. Many passport holders can obtain a visa on arrival at Gongbei Port for short stays. Check our detailed visa guide for specific requirements based on your citizenship.

Crossing time varies significantly: 15-30 minutes during off-peak hours, 1-2 hours during normal periods, and 3+ hours during holidays or weekends. Using automated facial recognition lanes can reduce your time by up to 50%.

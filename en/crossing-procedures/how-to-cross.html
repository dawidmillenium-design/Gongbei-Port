How to Cross Gongbei Port: Step-by-Step Guide

Complete walkthrough of the border crossing process including facial recognition channels, document requirements, and expert tips for a smooth experience.

Before You Start: Document Checklist

✓
Valid Passport

Must be valid for at least 6 months beyond your intended stay

✓
Visa (if required)

Check visa requirements based on your nationality. Many nationalities get visa-free entry to Macau for 30-90 days.

✓
Entry Permit for Mainland China

Required if entering Zhuhai from Macau (not needed for Macau entry from most countries)

✓
Completed Entry/Exit Cards

Available at the port or can be completed digitally in some cases

✓
Proof of Onward Travel

Return ticket or proof of exit from Macau/China may be requested

⚠️ Important Reminder

Always verify current visa requirements with official government sources before traveling. Regulations can change without notice.

Step-by-Step Crossing Process

Prepare Your Documents

Before approaching the border complex, ensure all your documents are easily accessible. Remove passports from protective covers, have visas unfolded, and keep entry cards ready.

  • Store documents in a waterproof, easily accessible pocket
  • Have digital copies saved on your phone as backup
  • Remove any passport covers or protective sleeves
  • Ensure visas are not expired and match your travel purpose

Enter the Port Complex

Follow clear signage indicating your direction of travel. Separate entrances exist for Zhuhai→Macau and Macau→Zhuhai flows.

  • Look for overhead signs in Chinese, Portuguese, and English
  • Choose the correct queue based on your traveler type (residents, tourists, staff)
  • Keep your boarding pass or transport tickets handy if arriving by train/bus

Security Screening

All travelers must pass through security checkpoints similar to airport screening.

  • Place bags, electronics, and metal objects in trays
  • Walk through metal detectors as instructed
  • Declare any prohibited items immediately
  • Follow instructions from security personnel promptly

Choose Your Immigration Channel

Select between manual counters staffed by officers or automated facial recognition channels (if eligible).

  • Manual Counters: Available to all travelers, recommended for first-time visitors or those with special circumstances
  • Automated Channels: Faster processing for eligible travelers with biometric passports
  • Look for illuminated signs indicating channel status (open/closed)

Immigration Processing

Present yourself at the chosen channel for document verification and biometric capture.

  • Manual Counter: Hand over passport and documents, answer officer's questions, provide fingerprints if requested
  • Automated Channel: Scan passport, look at camera for facial recognition, proceed when gates open
  • Wait for stamp/entry confirmation before moving forward
  • Collect all documents before leaving the counter area

Customs Declaration

After immigration, proceed through customs. Most travelers use the "Nothing to Declare" green channel.

  • Use green channel if carrying only personal effects within duty-free limits
  • Use red channel if carrying goods exceeding allowances or restricted items
  • Be prepared for random baggage inspections
  • Declare cash amounts over USD 5,000 (or equivalent)

Facial Recognition & Automated Channels

🤖 Using Automated Facial Recognition Channels

Gongbei Port features state-of-the-art automated border control systems that use facial recognition technology for faster processing. Here's everything you need to know:

Eligibility Requirements

  • Holders of biometric e-passports from eligible countries
  • Travelers aged 7-70 (children under 7 and seniors over 70 must use manual counters)
  • No previous immigration violations or watchlist flags
  • First-time users may need to register biometric data at manual counters

How to Use Automated Channels

  1. Approach the automated gate when the green light is illuminated
  2. Place your biometric passport face-down on the scanner
  3. Look directly at the camera when prompted (remove glasses, masks, hats)
  4. Wait for facial recognition match (usually takes 5-10 seconds)
  5. Proceed through the gate when it opens automatically
  6. Collect your passport from the output tray

💡 Tips for Success

  • Ensure good lighting on your face
  • Stand still during the scanning process
  • If recognition fails twice, proceed to manual counter
  • Keep hair away from eyes and remove heavy makeup if possible

Expert Tips for Smooth Crossing

⏰ Timing Strategy

Avoid peak hours (7-9 AM, 6-8 PM) when possible. Mid-morning (10 AM-12 PM) and early afternoon (2-4 PM) typically have shorter queues.

📱 Digital Preparation

Save digital copies of all documents on your phone. Download offline maps and translation apps as backup.

🎒 Luggage Management

Use wheeled bags for easy movement through queues. Keep valuables in a separate, easily accessible bag for security screening.

🗣️ Communication

Learn basic phrases in Mandarin/Cantonese. Most officers speak some English, but having key phrases ready helps.

💳 Payment Readiness

Have local currency (CNY/MOP) ready for any fees. Credit cards may not be accepted at all points.

🔄 Alternative Routes

If Gongbei is extremely crowded, consider Hengqin Port or Lotus Bridge as alternatives depending on your destination in Macau.

Common Issues & Solutions

❌ Problem: Facial Recognition Fails

Solution: Don't panic. After two failed attempts, the system will direct you to a manual counter. This is common for first-time users or if lighting conditions are poor. Proceed calmly to the officer.

❌ Problem: Missing Documentation

Solution: If you realize you're missing a document before reaching the counter, there are service desks near the entrance that can assist with emergency permits or provide guidance. Factor in extra time for this process.

✅ Success: Fast-Track Options

Frequent travelers can apply for registered traveler programs that provide access to expedited lanes. Check with immigration authorities in both Zhuhai and Macau for eligibility and application procedures.

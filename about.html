About this learning hub

Gongbei Port Learning Hub is an independent collection of visitor guides for the Zhuhai–Macao border. It is not an immigration authority, customs office, or official checkpoint website. The project is maintained through the public source repository. The pages do not currently identify a verified on-site reporter or claim firsthand inspection.

Editorial standards

  • Use official Macao and mainland China sources for operational, entry, and customs claims.
  • Link to the specific authority near time-sensitive statements when a claim is reviewed.
  • Show a fact-check date only after an actual review. A file change or design update is not a fact-check.
  • Mark translations and cross-language links clearly; do not present English pages as translated pages.
  • Remove or correct unsupported claims and unavailable resources rather than publishing placeholders as finished guides.

Official starting points

  • Macao Government Tourism Office: Border Gate and published hours
  • Macao SAR Government: immigration clearance for non-residents
  • National Immigration Administration of China
  • Macao Government Tourism Office: travelling to Macao

Before travelling: Confirm the latest checkpoint hours, entry eligibility for your passport, and customs rules with the relevant authorities. Border operations can change at short notice.

Corrections

If a link is broken or a statement is inaccurate, you can report it in the repository with the page URL and an official source. This editorial page was added on 23 September 2026; that date does not mean every guide was fact checked.
